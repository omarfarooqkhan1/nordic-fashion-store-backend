# Size Guide Template

This document provides guidelines for creating size guide images for products.

## Recommended Dimensions
- **Size**: 1200 x 1600 pixels (3:4 aspect ratio)
- **Format**: PNG or JPG
- **File Size**: Under 500 KB

## Template Structure

### Header Section
- **Brand Logo**: Nordic Fashion Store logo (top left or center)
- **Title**: "SIZE GUIDE" in large, bold text
- **Product Category**: e.g., "Leather Jackets", "Bags", "Belts"

### Measurement Instructions

Include a diagram showing:
1. **How to measure chest**: Measure around the fullest part of the chest
2. **How to measure waist**: Measure around the natural waistline
3. **How to measure shoulder**: Measure from shoulder point to shoulder point
4. **How to measure sleeve**: Measure from shoulder to wrist
5. **How to measure length**: Measure from highest point of shoulder to hem

### Additional Information

- **Fit Guide**: 
  - Slim Fit: Close to body
  - Regular Fit: Comfortable, not tight
  - Relaxed Fit: Loose and comfortable

- **Care Instructions**: Brief care tips
- **Material Info**: Leather type, lining material
- **Contact**: "Need help? Contact us at support@nordflex.store"

## Design Guidelines

### Colors
- **Background**: White or light gray (#F5F5F5)
- **Text**: Dark gray or black (#333333)
- **Accent**: Gold (#D4AF37) for highlights
- **Table**: Light borders (#E0E0E0)

### Typography
- **Title**: Bold, 48-60pt
- **Headers**: Semi-bold, 24-32pt
- **Body Text**: Regular, 14-18pt
- **Table Text**: Regular, 12-16pt

### Layout
- **Margins**: 60-80px on all sides
- **Spacing**: Consistent padding between sections
- **Alignment**: Center-aligned title, left-aligned tables

## Tools for Creating Size Guides

1. **Canva**: Easy-to-use templates
   - Search for "Size Chart" templates
   - Customize with your measurements
   - Export as PNG or JPG

2. **Adobe Photoshop/Illustrator**: Professional design
   - Create custom layouts
   - Use layers for easy editing
   - Export at high resolution

3. **Figma**: Free design tool
   - Collaborative design
   - Easy to share and edit
   - Export in multiple formats

4. **Google Slides/PowerPoint**: Quick and simple
   - Use tables for measurements
   - Add images and diagrams
   - Export as image

## Quick Start Template

For a quick start, you can:

1. Use Canva's "Size Chart" template
2. Replace measurements with your product measurements
3. Add your brand logo and colors
4. Export as 1200x1600px PNG
5. Upload to product in admin panel

## Best Practices

- ✅ Use clear, easy-to-read fonts
- ✅ Include both metric (cm) and imperial (inches) measurements
- ✅ Add visual diagrams showing where to measure
- ✅ Keep design clean and uncluttered
- ✅ Use consistent formatting across all size guides
- ✅ Test readability on mobile devices
- ✅ Update size guides if product specifications change
